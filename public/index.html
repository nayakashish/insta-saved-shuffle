Saved Shuffle

Your saved reels, in an order you did not pick.

Instagram keeps your saves in one long list you never scroll back through. Hand this page your own data export and it deals them out at random, or newest first, one at a time.

Import report

    Getting the export

    1. In Instagram, open Settings, then Accounts Center, then Your information and permissions.
    2. Choose Download your information, then Some of your information, and tick Saved.
    3. Pick Download to device, format HTML, date range All time.
    4. Instagram emails you when it is ready, usually within the hour. Download the ZIP and drop it above.

    Where your data goes

    Nowhere. The export is read and stored in this browser only. There is no server, no account and no upload, and you can erase everything from the library panel at any time.

    Videos play through Instagram's own public embed player, so Instagram can see which reels this browser loads, the same as when a reel is embedded on any website.

    Keeping it current

    Saved more since last time? Request a fresh export and import it again. New saves are added; what you have already watched, hidden or starred stays as it is.
