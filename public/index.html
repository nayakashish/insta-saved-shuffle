    Getting the export

    1. In Instagram, open Settings, then Accounts Center, then Your information and permissions.
    2. Choose Download your information, then Some of your information, and tick Saved.
    3. Pick Download to device, format HTML, date range All time.
    4. Instagram emails you when it is ready, usually within the hour. Download the ZIP and drop it above.

    Where your data goes

    Nowhere. The export is read and stored in this browser only. There is no account and no upload. Read the privacy notes.

    Keeping it current

    Saved more since last time? Request a fresh export and import it again. New saves are added; what you have watched, hidden or starred stays as it is.

    Privacy, plainly.

    This is a page that runs in your browser. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies of its own.

    What is kept, and where

    When you import an Instagram export, this page reads it on your device and keeps a list of your saves: each post's link, the account that posted it, its caption and hashtags, the date you saved it and the collection it is in. It also keeps what you do here: what you have watched, starred or hidden, which saves would not play, and your settings.

    All of that is stored in this browser's own storage for this site, on this device. It is not uploaded, synced or shared, and nobody running this site can see it. A different browser or device starts empty.

    What Instagram sees

    Videos are played by Instagram's own embedded player. To show a reel, your browser asks instagram.com for it directly, the same as when a reel is embedded in a news article. Instagram therefore sees your IP address and browser, may recognise you if you are signed in to Instagram in this browser, and may count a view. To make moving between reels quick, the next two reels are requested ahead of time, so Instagram may see those requests even if you stop before reaching them.

    This page never signs in to Instagram, never asks for your password and does not use Instagram's API or scrape anything.

    A list that came with the site

    Some copies of this site are set up with a list already loaded so there is nothing to import. That list belongs to whoever runs that copy, and anyone who can open the site can see it. If you import your own export it replaces that list in your browser only; your import is never sent back to the server.

    Topics

    Sorting saves into topics is done on your device by matching hashtags and caption words against a fixed word list. Nothing is sent to any service to do it.

    Offline copy

    So the page opens without a connection and can be installed, your browser keeps a copy of the page's own files. That copy contains no personal data.

    Removing everything

    Settings, then Erase all data, deletes the list and everything else stored here. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings does the same.

    Other people's posts

    Captions and account names belong to the people who posted them. This page stores only what your own export contains and shows posts only through Instagram's player, with a link back to the original. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta.
